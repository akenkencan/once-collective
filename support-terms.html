ONCE / SUPPORT TERMS

Supporting the work

Recipient: Alex Kennedy trading as ONCE Collective. ONCE is currently Alex’s creative practice, rather than an incorporated company or registered charity.

Your payment voluntarily supports Alex’s creative work, including films, gatherings and cultural experiments. It does not buy a card, product, membership, access, shares, repayment or any financial return. No Gift Aid or charitable tax relief is offered. Alex is not VAT registered and no VAT is added to these contributions.

Alex decides how to use unrestricted support across the creative practice. Future plans may change; they are not deliverables purchased by this contribution. Any separately agreed commission, artwork purchase or restricted funding arrangement needs its own terms.

Payment and questions

Stripe handles payment details on its secure checkout page. If your contribution was a mistake, was unauthorised or raises a concern, contact Alex through the website’s contact options. Requests will be reviewed individually. Nothing here limits any rights that apply by law.

Your information

Payment processing uses your contact and payment details to complete the contribution, issue payment records and respond to payment queries. The website stores a payment reference, amount, status, accepted terms version and dates. It does not store your full card details. A private acknowledgment task may be created so Alex can follow up personally.

Submitting a payment does not sign you up for marketing or make your name, story or amount public. Accounting records are retained where required for tax and legal obligations; other information is kept only as needed for the stated purpose. Contact Alex to ask about access, correction or deletion, subject to those obligations. You may also raise privacy concerns with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Support terms version: support-2026-10-26. Sandbox transactions use test details only.
